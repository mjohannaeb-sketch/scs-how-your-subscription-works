Questions we hear most

If yours is not here, our Customer Happiness Team is a phone call away.

How do I change my card?

Sign in to your Customer Account, open Payment methods, and select Add a payment method. Enter the new card and select Save card. Your subscription uses it from the next order onward, and if a payment was pending, we try it again for you. The full walkthrough is further up this page.

How do I pause my subscription?

Open Subscriptions, choose the product, and select Pause subscription. You choose whether it stops for one, two or three months, and it restarts on its own on the date shown. Your price and products are saved exactly as they are, and you can bring it back sooner at any time.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Subscriptions, choose the product, and select Cancel subscription. It takes effect straight away, with no call or email needed. If you only want a break, holding your deliveries keeps your subscriber price, which cancelling does not.

Where do I manage my subscription?

In your Customer Account at help.suncoastsciences.com. Sign in with the email address you used to order, and everything on this page is a click or two away.

When am I charged, and can I move the date?

You are charged each time a new order is created, on the schedule you chose. Nothing is taken in advance and there are no hidden fees. Before that date you can skip the shipment, change how often it arrives, or ship it early.

My card was declined. What now?

Usually the card on file has expired or your bank replaced it. Add the new card under Payment methods and the order goes through on the next attempt. Nothing else is needed from you.

Do I pay for shipping?

Shipping is free on orders over $50.

What if the product is not working for me?

Every order is covered by our 365-day guarantee. Call or email the team and they will take care of the return and the refund.
